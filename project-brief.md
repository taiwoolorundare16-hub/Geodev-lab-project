# My GIS project brief 

## My question
To what extent are primary healthcare facilities geographically accessible to people living in Kano State, Nigeria 

## The data i need 
-Health facilities - GRID3 Nigeria - http:data.grid3.org - point data Geopackage or CSV.
-Population date - worldPop - https://www.worldpop.org - Raster GeoTIFF.
-State and LGA boundaries - GRID3 or UN SALB - https://data.grid3.org - Vector - Geopackage or Shapefile. 
-Ward boundaries - GRIDS Nigeria - http://data.grid3.org - Vector - Geopackage.
-Roads and walking paths - OpenStreetMap - https://www.openstreetmap.org - Vector - Geopackage or Shapefile.
